THE PILLARS

This is the Men's Ministry.

Our Vision

We visualize developing men who will walk and their reach the full potential in Christ.

To have men who will also love and lead their families on the path of the Our God Jehovah.

Our Mission

To have men who will:

  • Love God wholeheartedly

    • Luke 10:27 - He answered, “ ‘Love the Lord your God with all your
    heart and with all your soul and with all your strength and with all your mind’ ; and, ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’

  • Be strong and courageous in God.

    • Joshua 1:9 - Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous.
    Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the LORD your God will be with you wherever you go.

  • Be humble.

    • 1 Peter 5:5 - In the same way, you who are younger, submit yourselves
    to your elders. All of you, clothe yourselves with humility toward one another, because, “God opposes the proud but shows favor to the humble.
