Pre-Marital Counselling

Here in DC-Magomano, we believe that a marriage should involve three parties-the man, woman and of course, God since He is the one who created it.

Matthew 19:6 - So they are no longer two but one flesh. What therefore God has joined together, let not man separate.


The premarital counseling is a guided preparation process for engaged couples led by the pastor to know how if they are compatible with each other. This is a valuable step to build a Christ-centered foundation.

We would appreciate if the questions are answered with honesty and intergrity.
