Our Vision

Give our lives to Christ Jesus.

Our Mission

It is for our youth to:

  • Start building themselves up as someone worth admiring by others

    • Timothy 4:12 - Don't let anyone look down on you because you are young, but set an example for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith and in purity.

  • Know that no matter the size or strength, it’s possible to tackle even the most intimidating of foes.

    • Samuel 17:42 - And when the Philistine looked and saw David, he disdained him, for he was but a youth, ruddy and handsome in appearance.

  • Staying true to your parents and not disrespecting them.

    • Proverbs 1:8-9 - Hear, my son, your father’s instruction, and forsake not your mother’s teaching, for they are a graceful garland for your head and pendants for your neck.
