CHILDREN'S AND TEENS' MINISTRY

This ministry is for minors (below 18 years)

Our Vision

It is raise children and teens grow to know God and love themselves in Christ.

Our Mission

It is to help them:

  • To love and respect their parents.

    • Colossians 3:20 - Children, obey your parents in everything, for this pleases the Lord.

  • To know that they are made God's image.

    • Psalm 139:14 - I praise you, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made. Wonderful are your works; my soul knows it very well.
