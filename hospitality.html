HOSPITALITY MINISTRY

This ministry is to make people feel welcomed.

Our Vision

It is to help guests feel and experience the love of God by truly welcoming them into God’s house through friendly and authentically gracious service.

Our Mission

It is to:

  • Serve Jesus Himself

    • Matthew 25:40 - Whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me.

  • Welcome Strangers

    • Hebrews 13:2 - Do not forget to show hospitality to strangers, for by so doing some people have shown hospitality to angels without knowing it.

  • Show No Partiality.

    • Luke 14:13-14 - When you give a banquet, invite the poor, the crippled, the lame, the blind, and you will be blessed.
