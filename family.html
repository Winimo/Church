FAMILY LIFE

Baby Dedication Class

The arrival of a new baby is time for rejoicing for any family. The newness of life often brings a special freshness toward all of life. Often parents want to do something in the church building to celebrate their joy with others and present their child to God for blessing.


At DC-Magomano, we wholeheartedly believe that children are a gift from God and as parents, the stewardship of that gift carries great joy and responsibility.

Samuel 1:11 - …then I will give him to the LORD all the days of his life.


If you are a parent and would like to dedicate your child, we encourage you to attend the Class.
