THE ESTHERS

There are two groups in the Esthers:

  • The Deborahs

    • These are ladies who are 35 years and above.

  • The Lydias

    • These are ladies who are below 35 years old.

Our Vision

Our vision to have ladies who will grow their faith in God.

Ladies who will be live a life of Honesty and Integrity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to have women who will:

  • Have guidance and faith

    • Psalm 46:5 - God is within her, she will not fall; God will help her at break of day.

  • Have strength and unity

    • Genesis 1:27 - So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them.(Worth and equality in God's eye)

  • Be loyal

    • Ruth 1:16-17 - But Ruth said, 'Do not urge me to leave you or to turn back from you. Where you go I will go, and where you stay I will stay. Your people will be my people and your God my God. Where you die I will die, and there I will be buried. May the Lord do so to me, and more also, if anything but death separates me from you.
